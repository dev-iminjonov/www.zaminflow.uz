# Defect Report: QA-004

**Title:** Emerald Brand Green Text (`#00C853` and `#22d36b`) Fails WCAG 2.2 Contrast on Light Glass (< 3:1)  
**Defect Type:** Accessibility Defect / Visual Contrast (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum)  
**Severity:** S2 Major  
**Priority:** P1  
**Status:** New  
**Area:** Public Landing Page / Design System Tokens  
**Components:** [`HeroEcosystemVisual.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/Muhammad%20Aziz/Downloads/www.zaminflow.uz/frontend/src/components/HeroEcosystemVisual.tsx#L47), [`OurTeamCarousel.tsx`](file:///c:/Users/Muhammad%20Aziz/Downloads/www.zaminflow.uz/frontend/src/components/OurTeamCarousel.tsx#L155)  

---

### Preconditions
Page viewed in standard Light Mode on desktop or mobile in outdoor or bright lighting conditions.

---

### Steps to Reproduce
1. Navigate to `http://localhost:3000/`.
2. Inspect the floating frosted cards in the Hero ecosystem visual:
   - "Live Pricing" label: color `#00C853` on background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
   - "Logistics" label: color `#00C853` on background `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.92)`
   - "Auto-release upon titration" label: color `#00C853`
3. Measure the luminance contrast ratio using a calibrated color contrast analyzer or Chrome DevTools.

---

### Expected Result (WCAG 2.2 SC 1.4.3 Contrast Minimum)
Normal text (< 18pt regular or < 14pt bold) must have a contrast ratio of at least **4.5:1** against its background. Large text (>= 18pt or >= 14pt bold) must have at least **3:1**.

---

### Actual Result
* Color `#00C853` against `#FFFFFF` yields a contrast ratio of **2.43:1** (Fails both 4.5:1 and 3:1).
* Color `#22d36b` against `#FFFFFF` yields a contrast ratio of **1.78:1** (Fails both 4.5:1 and 3:1).
* Text rendered with these colors is washed out and illegible for users with low vision or in bright mobile environments.

---

### Reproducibility
Static design token measurement: 100% reproducible.

---

### Suggested Fix
Use the approved accessible green text token `#087a3c` (contrast **5.25:1**) for all readable text, labels, and icons on white/mint backgrounds, reserving `#00C853` and `#22d36b` strictly for glowing backgrounds, active pills with dark text, or decorative vector line art:
