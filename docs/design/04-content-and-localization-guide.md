## 1. Domain Terminology Glossary

| English Term | Uzbek Latin (`uz`) | Russian (`ru`) | Plain-Language Definition for Farmers & Buyers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Raw Milk** | Xom sut | Сырое молоко | Unpasteurized, unprocessed whole cow milk directly from dairy farm cooling tank. |
| **Titration Acidity** | Titrlanadigan kislotalilik (${}^\circ\text{T}$) | Титруемая кислотность (${}^\circ\text{T}$) | Measure of milk freshness expressed in Turner degrees (${}^\circ\text{T}$). Standard milk is $\le 18.0^\circ\text{T}$. |
| **Fat Content** | Yog'lilik darajasi (%) | Массовая доля жира (%) | Percentage of butterfat in milk. Baseline quality standard is $3.60\%$. |
| **Solids-Not-Fat (SNF)**| Quruq yog'siz sut qoldig'i (QYSQ / SOMO) | Сухой обезжиренный молочный остаток (СОМО) | Total solids excluding milk fat (proteins, lactose, minerals). Standard is $8.50\%$. |
| **Escrow Lock** | Eskrou muzlatish | Эскроу блокировка | Funds securely locked in partner bank account prior to pickup; released automatically upon titration verification. |
| **Factoring Payout** | Tezkor faktoring to'lovi | Факторинговая выплата | Immediate bank advance payment (typically 98.5%) to farmer prior to the dairy's standard 30-day payment term. |
| **Cold-Chain** | Sovuq zanjir logistikasi | Холодовая цепь | Continuous refrigeration transport ($\le 4.0^\circ\text{C}$ to $8.0^\circ\text{C}$) preventing milk spoilage. |
| **Arbitration Vial** | Hakamlik sinov namunasi | Арбитражная проба | Sealed reference milk sample collected at farm gate, used to resolve quality disputes. |

## 2. Formatting Standards

- **Currency:** Uzbek Som formatted with space separation and `UZS` suffix: `4 500 UZS/L` or `45 000 000 UZS` (no decimals for UZS in public circulation).
- **Date & Time:**
  - Date: `DD.MM.YYYY` (e.g. `07.11.2026`)
  - Time: 24-hour format with Tashkent timezone `UTC+5` (e.g. `07:30 Tashkent`).
- **Temperature:** Celsius with single decimal: `+3.8 °C`.
- **Acidity:** Turner degrees: `16.5 °T`.
