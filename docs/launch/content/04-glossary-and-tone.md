## 1. Tone of Voice & Editorial Principles

ZaminFlow operates at the intersection of agriculture, industrial processing, and fintech in Uzbekistan. Our content must communicate:
1. **Clarity & Dignity for Farmers:** Plain language, no condescending jargon, no convoluted finance buzzwords. A farmer using an Android phone in Fergana should understand payment terms and milk grading instantly.
2. **Industrial Precision for Commercial Dairies:** Objective quality parameters (fat %, SNF %, titration degrees °T, cold-chain temperature logs).
3. **Institutional Trust:** Absolute transparency on escrow mechanics, dispute arbitration, and fees. No exaggerated marketing claims.

## 2. Standardized Trilingual Glossary

| English Term | Russian (ru) | Uzbek Latin (uz) | Definition & Contextual Rule |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Raw Milk** | Сырое молоко | Xom sut | Unprocessed, fresh cow's milk prior to industrial pasteurization. |
| **Batch / Shipment** | Партия молока | Sut partiyasi | A distinct volume of milk collected from a farm and transported by tanker. |
| **Fat Content** | Массовая доля жира | Yogʻlilik darajasi | Percentage of butterfat (standard benchmark: 3.60%). Note the apostrophe `oʻ`. |
| **SNF (Solids-Not-Fat)** | СОМО (сухой обезжиренный молочный остаток) | QAYQ (yogʻsiz quruq qoldiq) | Protein, lactose, and minerals excluding water and butterfat (standard: 8.50%). |
| **Titration Acidity** | Титруемая кислотность (°Т) | Titrlangan kislotalilik (°T) | Freshness measurement expressed in Thörner degrees (max standard: 18.00 °T). |
| **Escrow Account** | Эскроу-счёт | Eskrou hisobvaragʻi | Segregated account holding buyer funds until milk passes laboratory titration. |
| **Factoring** | Факторинг | Faktoring | Early payout of receivables against delivered milk batches for working capital. |
| **Farm-Gate Price** | Базовая цена с фермы | Fermer narxi | Agreed base price per liter before quality premiums or temperature penalties. |
| **Cold-Chain** | Холодовая цепь | Sovutish zanjiri | Unbroken temperature-controlled logistics maintaining raw milk at $\le 4.0^\circ\text{C}$. |
| **Titration Verification** | Приёмка по титрации | Titratsiya boʻyicha qabul | Final quality check at dairy intake gate that unlocks payment release. |
| **Settlement** | Взаиморасчёты | Yakuniy hisob-kitob | Reconciliation and bank transfer execution for accepted deliveries. |
| **Arbitration / Dispute** | Арбитраж разногласий | Nizolarni hal qilish | Process invoked if dairy lab test diverges by $>0.2\%$ from farm baseline. |

## 3. Formatting & Style Rules

1. **Uzbek Typography:**
   - Always use the correct modifier letter turned comma for `oʻ` (`U+02BB` or standard single quote `'`) and `gʻ`.
   - Never use backticks (\`) or Russian accents (ó, ѓ).
2. **Currency & Numbers:**
   - Uzbek Som: always formatted with non-breaking spaces as thousands separators.
     - English: `4,380 UZS / L` or `12,500,000 UZS`
     - Russian: `4 380 сум/л` or `12 500 000 сум`
     - Uzbek: `4 380 soʻm/l` or `12 500 000 soʻm`
   - Volume: compact format for large public numbers (`4.2M L`), full numbers with space separation for invoices (`4 200 000 L`).
3. **Date and Time:**
   - Calendar Day definition: **Asia/Tashkent** (UTC+5).
   - Time format: 24-hour clock (`08:30`, `18:00`).
   - Date format: `DD.MM.YYYY` or `7-noyabr, 2026-yil` in Uzbek prose.
